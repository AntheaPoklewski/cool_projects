Intra-vehicle Coffee Stabiliser and Anti-Spill Device

University of Pretoria · Electronic Engineering · 1st place final-year project

A sensor-driven platform that keeps a cup level under normal driving conditions — braking, accelerating, and turning — using IMU data, orientation estimation, and PID control on a 3-RRS parallel manipulator.

Overview

The goal was to prevent coffee spilling in a moving vehicle under normal driving conditions. The system combines a sensor suite (inertial measurement unit) and orientation estimation algorithm, a control subsystem (PID controller) on an embedded device, and a 3-RRS parallel manipulator (3D-printed platform and servo motors).

Code, CAD, and control scripts are in the automated liquid control folder.

System building blocks

  • Sensing — IMU for vehicle motion
  • Estimation — orientation / attitude from sensor data
  • Control — PID on an STM32 embedded platform
  • Actuation — 3-RRS parallel manipulator with servo motors
