Line-Following Robot

Ice-cream-truck themed line following robot built for Robot Race Day — sensing the track, steering along the line, and surviving race-day excitement.

Overview

We built a line-following robot for the EMK 310 Robot Race Day in 2022. The robot was designed to follow a green, red, blue or black line on a white track, navigating turns and track crossings. We used a combination of phototransistor sensors to detect the line, a PIC18 microcontroller to process the sensor data and bang-bang control for the motors. The chassis was made of veroboard, onto which our components were soldered. We decorated the robot to look like an ice cream truck, with a white and pink color scheme, and a small ice cream cone on top.

Our robot performed moderately well in the race, successfully following the line and completing the course, however it was quite slow, taking 2 minutes to complete the track. We pulled two all-nighters to get the robot race-day ready, but despite all this, Race Day was still lots of fun!
