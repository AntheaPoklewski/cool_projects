SLAM and Visual Reconstruction in Outdoor Environments

Master’s work on simultaneous localisation and mapping (SLAM), and 3D reconstruction, for elephant impact monitoring in the Kruger National Park.

Overview

My research work is focused on using visual sensors to perform change detection in outdoor environments for conservation efforts. I am approaching the end of my first year of study, with one year remaining!

Project challenges

Outdoor environments are challenging SLAM systems as the inconsistent lighting, vegetation, scale, and motion can cause issues with feature detection and tracking. I am exploring the use of a quadruped robot to traverse the terrain, and using visual-inertial odometry to estimate the robot’s pose. The goal is to build a map of the environment, and then use that map to detect changes over time.

More to come as I continue my research and development of the system, and I will be updating this page with my progress. Stay tuned!
